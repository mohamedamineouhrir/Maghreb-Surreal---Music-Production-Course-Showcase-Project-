THE APPROACH

HOW WE TEACH.

I

Character Before Technicality

We let you experience the magic before we explain the math. You hear a Minimoog before you learn what an oscillator is. You feel the character of a vintage CS-80 before you understand what a filter envelope does. Curiosity is the engine. Technicality is the fuel that follows.

II

Progress-Dependent, Not Calendar-Dependent

There are no fixed 3-month deadlines. You advance when you are ready — not when a calendar says so. Each phase has clear, demonstrable exit criteria: you can play a full song from theory, you can design a sound from scratch, you can deliver a clean mix. Mastery is the only clock.

III

Collective Practice, Individual Vision

Weekly group sessions deconstruct real records together. You are never alone in the formation. But the goal is never conformity — it is to give every student the technical vocabulary to realize their own specific artistic world, whatever that world sounds like.

IV

Unlearn the YouTube Rulebook

Most production advice online is context-free and dogmatic. "Always high-pass at 80 Hz." "Compress everything with a 4:1 ratio." We replace rigid rules with flexible principles. A mixing decision is only correct inside a specific context — you learn to think, not to follow instructions blindly.
